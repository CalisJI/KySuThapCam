On **November 3, 2026**, IoT Now reported that **Ceva and Mobilint** integrated Ceva's **SensPro AI DSP** and **NeuPro-Nano NPU** into Mobilint's **REGULUS Edge AI system-on-chip**.

According to the companies' announcement, REGULUS delivers **10 TOPS** of AI compute performance at **under 3W**, with support for up to **8GB of memory**. It targets AI inference on devices rather than sending every sensor reading or video frame to the cloud.

## Why does on-device AI matter?

Continuously transmitting sensor data and video to remote servers consumes bandwidth, introduces latency and can raise data-privacy concerns.

Edge AI enables certain tasks—such as object detection, video analysis and sensor-driven responses—to run locally. Potential applications include **smart cameras, industrial machine vision, robots, drones and power-constrained IoT devices**.

## What's inside REGULUS?

- **SensPro AI DSP:** programmable sensing and signal-processing capabilities.
- **NeuPro-Nano NPU:** energy-efficient neural-network inference acceleration.
- **Multi-engine architecture:** assigns different sensing and AI workloads to suitable processing engines.

REGULUS also powers Mobilint's commercially available **MLD-R1 AI USB accelerator**, intended to add local AI processing to existing platforms.

## Potential applications in Vietnamese factories

Potential uses include conveyor-based defect detection, product counting, safety-zone monitoring and local video analytics at the edge. Real deployment depends on model accuracy, supported frameworks, memory needs and inference optimization.

**Important:** 10 TOPS is a manufacturer-reported compute metric. It does **not** guarantee a particular inference speed or accuracy for every model or workload.

A compact AI vision station can be a valuable retrofit when manual inspection creates a production bottleneck or misses costly defects.

## Official video demo

[**IDS NXT — AI-based quality inspection**](https://www.ids-imaging.us/visionchannel-media-details/items/ai-based-quality-inspection.html)

The video demonstrates snap-ring checks, wood inspection, bottle-top identification and AI vision workflows.

## System architecture

Part-present sensor triggers a camera → controlled lighting and imaging → AI inference → OK/NG signal to PLC → diverter, reject station or alarm.

Start with **one economically important defect** and collect representative accepted and rejected examples.

## Validate before production

Lighting, positioning, trigger timing and line speed all affect results. Measure false accepts and false rejects against independently held-out production samples.

Total electricity bills cannot show **which production line spends the most energy per good unit**. A useful retrofit joins energy metering with production data.

## Watch the demo

[**Muvia — Energy use per line and per piece**](https://muviabi.com/en/industrial/use-cases/energy)

The demonstration uses actual product screens with **sample data**, not independently verified customer savings.

## A small-factory architecture

Three-phase energy meters or power analysers → Modbus gateway → PLC production counters → shifts/orders → dashboards for kWh per line, shift and good unit.

An initial KPI is **kWh per accepted unit** over an aligned time interval. Mixed-product production requires appropriate energy allocation between orders.

## Implementation checks

Separate idle and active energy, recover meter counters after network outages and compare equivalent production conditions.

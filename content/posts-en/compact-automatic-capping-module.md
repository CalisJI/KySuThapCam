When one manual station becomes a bottleneck, the right investment may be **a standalone automation module**, not a completely new line.

## Watch the original machine demo

[**CM3 Engineering — automatic twist-off capping case study and video**](https://www.cm3engineering.com/en/automatic-capping-with-conveyor-belt-for-twist-off-caps/)

CM3 describes a compact conveyor-fed capping system using a pneumatic screwdriver and HMI recipe settings for glass jars.

## Integration concept

Filled jars enter the conveyor, are positioned for capping and closed according to the selected recipe. The module can be integrated after an existing filling machine.

## Practical design checks

Assess line speed, container formats, cap tolerances, hygiene, floor space, upstream/downstream handshaking, jam detection and changeover procedures.

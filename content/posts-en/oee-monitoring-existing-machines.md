Many factories operate reliable equipment yet cannot quantify **runtime, idle time or downtime**. Monitoring can often begin without replacing the machine or PLC.

## Watch the demonstration

[**Official Zapsi MES video demonstration**](https://arcelormittal.zapsi.eu/en/info)

The vendor's walkthrough covers machine overview, production, downtime, energy and OEE indicators.

## A practical small-scale retrofit

Start with a small group of CNC machines, presses or packaging units. Read available RUN/STOP signals safely, capture production counts and timestamp the data at an edge gateway. Record downtime reasons from PLC faults where available or through an operator interface.

OEE combines **Availability × Performance × Quality**. Runtime signals alone are not enough to calculate a complete, reliable OEE measure.

## Engineering checks

Validate downtime against operator logs, distinguish planned stops from breakdowns, and isolate OT connectivity appropriately.
